    Responsive Reflow Rules

    1Sidebar dissolves. Its 8 modules are redistributed inline between content sections — Most Read after Regional, Poll after Politics, Newsletter before Footer. Nothing is dropped.
    2Grids collapse predictably. 4-col → 2-col at 768px → 1-col at 480px. The 14 content hubs become a 2-col grid, then a horizontal swipe rail — never a 14-item vertical stack.
    3Bottom tab bar replaces the 12-item nav. Five destinations only: होम · विभाग · व्हिडिओ · शिवसेना कॉर्नर · शोध. Full category tree lives behind the hamburger.
    4Web Stories move up. On mobile they sit directly under the hero — highest tap-through of any module for under-30 readers. On desktop they stay lower.
    5Ad reserved space is fixed at every breakpoint so late-loading creatives never shift text. This is what protects the CLS score and Discover eligibility.
